Assessment redesign

Three prototypes used during the redesign

These prototypes show how the assessment direction developed through assessor and school testing, and the version now intended to be taken forward into Alpha.

Prototype 1

Exploring assessment approaches with assessors

Purpose: To explore different ways of structuring the assessment and understand the trade-offs between transparency, consistency, reflection and school voice.

Audience: Artsmark assessors.

Open prototype 1

Prototype 2

Testing the emerging assessment with schools

Purpose: To test the emerging assessment structure with re-applying schools through survey feedback and small-group sessions, including how clear, usable and representative the form felt.

Audience: Teachers and school leaders with recent Artsmark experience.

Open prototype 2

Prototype 3 · Current direction

Version to take forward into Alpha

Purpose: To bring together the learning from assessor and school testing into the current assessment direction, including separate enrolment, criterion-linked assessment sections and space for school voice and distinctiveness.

Audience: Artsmark schools, assessors and the team developing the Alpha service.

Open prototype 3

These are research and design prototypes rather than final service specifications. Prototype 3 represents the current direction at project handover and should continue to be tested and refined through Alpha.
