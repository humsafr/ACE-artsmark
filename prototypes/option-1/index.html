Before you begin

Tell the story of your provision through the Artsmark Framework

You will respond once against each of the eight Artsmark Framework criteria, then review the application as a whole and optionally add a small number of supporting items.

You do not need to describe everything your setting has done. Choose one or two concrete examples for each criterion that help an assessor understand your intention, current practice, impact and learning.

Focus on the essential

Explain what you intended to strengthen, what now happens in practice, the difference it has made and how you know. Be open about what you learned, adapted or still need to strengthen.

Specific examples from ordinary school life are more useful than broad or polished claims.
