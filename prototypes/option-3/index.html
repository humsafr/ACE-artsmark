Before you begin

Make an evidence-aware judgement about your current provision

This route is a structured self-assessment. You will choose the statements that best describe your current practice, add brief examples, and explain what your judgements are based on.

An assessor would then review whether the choices are supported, need qualification or require clarification. The questionnaire does not generate an award level automatically.

What makes this more than a checklist

Each criterion asks about both the depth of practice and how widely, consistently and sustainably that practice is established. You will also identify the basis for your judgement and add one short example for each section.
