Before you begin

Tell a coherent story about your arts and cultural provision

This version brings the eight Artsmark Framework criteria together into four connected narrative sections.

Each section asks two substantial questions. The prompts help you describe current practice, give examples, explain impact, and be open about challenges or areas that remain less developed.

The framework criteria remain visible throughout, but you can respond through a more joined-up account of how the work operates across your setting.

What you'll be asked to provide

You'll give long-form answers across four sections. At the end, you can optionally add up to three selected evidence items and one short spoken reflection. Both are optional and should only clarify or substantiate what is already in your written account.

      Optional supporting evidence

      Add selected evidence that substantiates your account

      Your written application should stand on its own. You may add up to three items only where they support a specific claim or help an assessor understand something more clearly.

      Evidence is optional. Choosing not to add any will not disadvantage your application, and the number or presentation quality of items is not assessed.

      What can be useful

      • a short curriculum or progression extract
      • pupil, staff or partner voice
      • participation or progression information
      • a brief leadership or improvement-planning extract
      • an example of pupil work with clear context

      Keep it selective

      • Choose only evidence that supports something already stated
      • Do not upload full policies, large reports or folders
      • Do not add unlabelled images or unrelated showcase material
      • Avoid unnecessary personal information

      What assessors need

      For each item, explain which part of the written application it supports and what the assessor should notice.

      Optional spoken reflection

      Add one short spoken reflection

      You can add one short recording to clarify something that is difficult to communicate fully in writing.

      This is optional. Choosing not to add a video will not disadvantage your application.

      The prompt

      Choose one question to answer

      • What has changed most significantly in your setting, and why does it matter?
      • What would you most like the assessor to understand about your context or journey?

      Use the recording to clarify something already present in your written application, not to introduce a separate body of evidence.

      Add your spoken reflection

      Accepted formats: MP4, MOV, WebM, MP3, M4A or WAV. Maximum two minutes. File-size limit to be confirmed.

      This helps the assessor understand what part of the written application the reflection relates to. 0 / 250 characters
      Prototype note: the file itself is not uploaded or retained. Only its name is shown during this browser session.

      Simple, comparable format

      • One member of staff speaking directly to camera
      • One take is enough
      • Maximum two minutes
      • Audio-only is also acceptable

      How this stays fair

      • No music, graphics or professional editing are needed
      • Do not film a school tour or showcase facilities
      • Do not include pupils or other identifiable people
      • Production quality and presentation skills are not assessed

      Before uploading

      • Make sure the content relates to the written application
      • Include captions or a transcript where relevant
      • Avoid unnecessary personal information

      In a live service, the recording would use an approved secure submission method. This prototype does not upload or retain media.
